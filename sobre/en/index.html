About OAlquimista

The art of cooking, in the palm of your hand.

Why OAlquimista exists

OAlquimista was built for anyone tired of losing recipes in scattered photos and screenshots. It's your personal digital recipe book — elegant, intelligent, and completely yours.

Organize, create, and explore recipes with the sophistication of a professional chef and the convenience of a modern app. From draft to table, OAlquimista is with you at every step: full recipe entries with photos and notes, search by ingredient or category, per-step timers in Cook Mode, one-tap shopping lists, and weekly meal planning.

It's a great fit for home cooks looking for more organization, families planning weekly meals, and food enthusiasts who love exploring new ingredients.

How it works

A flow designed from draft to table

📖

Add or import

Type your recipe or import it from a website, PDF, photo, or free text with Apple Intelligence — the AI automatically extracts ingredients and steps.

👨‍🍳

Cook with Cook Mode

Follow the full-screen step-by-step, with a per-step timer and real-time serving adjustments — never lose your rhythm.

🗓

Plan the week

Build your menu and generate the week's shopping list in one tap, straight from the recipes you picked.
