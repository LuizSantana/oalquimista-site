Delete account and data

Permanently deletes your OAlquimista account and all associated data: synced recipes, categories, collections, subscription, and AI usage history.

PT-BR PT-PT EN

smartphone Fastest: right in the app

If the app is still installed, delete your account instantly under Profile → Settings → Delete Account. No email, no wait.

mail No app installed: delete here

Enter your account's email (the same one used with Sign in with Apple or Google). We'll send a confirmation link — it expires in 1 hour and works once.

What gets deleted

  • Your account (Sign in with Apple / Google)
  • Recipes, categories, and collections synced via OAlquimista Plus
  • Subscription and AI usage history
  • Active sessions on every device

Recipes kept only on your personal iCloud (Plus sync not enabled) aren't touched by this process — delete the app to remove those, like any other Apple app. See the full Privacy Policy.
