What's New

What changed in each version of OAlquimista.

Version 1.0.0

June 13, 2026

First release

  • Recipe catalog — real-time search, grid or list view, a favorites carousel, and filters by category and tags.
  • Cook Mode — full-screen step-by-step that never sleeps, with a per-step timer and dynamic serving-size adjustment.
  • Intelligent import — import recipes from websites, camera (scanner + OCR), PDF, or free text using Apple Intelligence, which automatically extracts ingredients and steps.
  • Discover tab — a built-in browser to import from any site, plus recipe suggestions to explore.
  • Shopping list — generated from the ingredients of any recipe, with purchased items and text sharing.
  • Meal planner — organize breakfast, lunch, and dinner for the week and export to the iPhone Calendar.
  • Customizable categories and collections, with colors and icons.
  • Profile with recipe stats, favorites and cooked history, and iCloud sync status.
  • Widget showing your next planned meal.
  • iCloud sync across iPhone, iPad, and Mac.
