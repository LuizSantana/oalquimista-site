OAlquimista Privacy Policy

Effective: July 16, 2026

OAlquimista does not collect, transmit, or sell personal data to third parties. This notice describes exactly what stays on your device and what is synced through your own iCloud.

What we collect

The free features of OAlquimista — recipe library, Cook Mode, meal planner, and shopping list — don't require signup and don't collect personally identifiable data.

OAlquimista Plus (optional subscription) uses Sign in with Apple to validate your access. We only collect the anonymous identifier Apple provides in that process — we never ask for a separate password, and you can choose not to share your real name or email with us through Apple's private email relay.

The recipes you create — including title, ingredients, steps, notes, and photos — are your own content, stored locally on your device.

How we use this information

We don't use your recipes, photos, or notes for anything beyond displaying them back to you inside the app. There is no third-party analytics or behavioral tracking.

Where your data lives

All content you create in the app — recipes, photos, categories, collections, shopping lists, and meal plans — is stored locally on your device using iOS's SwiftData.

Optionally, this content is synced across your own devices (iPhone, iPad, and Mac) through your private iCloud (CloudKit), tied to your Apple ID and subject to Apple's own Privacy Policy. We never send your recipes to our own or any third-party servers.

If you subscribe to OAlquimista Plus, your account identifier and subscription status live on a small backend of our own, used solely to validate your access — never to store your recipe content, which stays in your personal iCloud.

Third-party services

OAlquimista doesn't use advertising SDKs or third-party trackers. Features like recipe import from camera, PDF, or text use on-device processing via Apple Intelligence — no content is sent to external servers for that analysis.

Advertising

OAlquimista does not show ads and does not share data with advertising networks.

Data retention

Aside from the account identifier and subscription status of OAlquimista Plus subscribers, we don't store copies of your data outside your device and your personal iCloud. Deleting the app removes all locally stored data; deleting your Plus account (see "Your rights" below) removes the rest.

Your rights

If you only use the free features, there's no personal data of yours for us to access, correct, or delete on our end. If you subscribe to OAlquimista Plus, you can delete your account and all associated data at any time, directly in the app — deletion is immediate and doesn't require contacting support, per the App Store's guidelines for apps with accounts.

Children

OAlquimista does not collect data from any user, regardless of age. The app is suitable for all ages (App Store rating 4+).

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page will always be updated when that happens.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy or how we handle your information, contact us at suporte@oalquimista.store.
